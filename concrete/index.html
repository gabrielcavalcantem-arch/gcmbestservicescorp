Frequently Asked

Questions worth asking.

Is stamped concrete a good fit for luxury homes?

When designed and finished properly, stamped and decorative concrete delivers a refined, custom look at scale. GCM specifies patterns, borders and lighting so the result reads architectural rather than generic.

How do you prevent cracking?

We build on a compacted base with correct control joints and reinforcement, engineered for Florida soil and climate, and back the work with a written guarantee.

Can concrete match my home's architecture?

Yes. We tailor color, texture and layout to the home's materials and lines during the design consultation.
