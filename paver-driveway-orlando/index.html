In Short

A driveway built for Orlando, not just in Orlando.

Orlando driveways fight three things: summer heat, afternoon downpours and a high water table. GCM builds every paver driveway on a compacted, engineered base with proper slope and edge restraint, so water leaves the surface, the pattern never shifts and the drive still looks intentional twenty years from now. We serve Dr. Phillips, Bay Hill, Windermere, Lake Nona, Winter Park and every Orlando neighborhood in between, and we handle HOA submittals for gated communities.

Frequently Asked

Questions worth asking.

How much does a paver driveway cost in Orlando?

Most paver driveways in Orlando start around $10 per square foot installed. Travertine, porcelain and intricate borders run higher. After a site visit you receive a fixed written quote — no surprises.

How long does a paver driveway take to install?

A typical two-car driveway takes 3 to 5 working days once materials arrive: demolition, base, laying, cutting, borders, sand and sealing.

Do pavers hold up better than concrete in Orlando?

Yes. Pavers flex with Florida soil instead of cracking, and any future repair is invisible because each piece is set individually. Concrete slabs crack and show every patch.

Do you handle HOA approval?

We do. GCM prepares the material and layout documents your HOA asks for and builds within gated communities with full discretion.
