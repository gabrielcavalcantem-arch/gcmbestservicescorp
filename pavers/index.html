Frequently Asked

Questions worth asking.

Which paver material is best for Florida?

Travertine and honed marble stay cooler underfoot and resist Florida sun and moisture, making them ideal for pool decks and estate driveways. Large-format porcelain is best where a modern, near-maintenance-free surface is the priority.

How long does a luxury paver installation last?

Built on a properly engineered base and sealed correctly, a high-end paver installation lasts for decades. Because pavers are individually set, any future repair stays invisible.

Do you work in gated communities?

Yes. We regularly build within Windermere, Isleworth, Keene's Pointe and other gated communities, handling HOA submittals and access with full discretion.
