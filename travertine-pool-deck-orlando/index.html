In Short

Cool underfoot in August. Dry in minutes after the storm.

Travertine is the pool-deck material for Central Florida for three reasons: it stays cool in direct sun, its natural texture is slip-resistant when wet, and it drains fast. GCM removes the old slab or pavers, re-grades the deck toward drains, sets travertine on a compacted base and finishes the pool edge with matching bullnose coping. Marble and large-format porcelain are available for a more modern look. We build pool decks across Orlando, Dr. Phillips, Windermere, Lake Nona and Winter Park.

Frequently Asked

Questions worth asking.

How much does a travertine pool deck cost in Orlando?

Travertine pool decks in Orlando start around $12 per square foot installed, including base work. Coping, demolition and drainage adjustments are quoted per project in a fixed written estimate.

Does travertine get hot in the Florida sun?

Far less than concrete or brick pavers. Travertine reflects heat and stays comfortable to walk on barefoot in the afternoon — the main reason Orlando homeowners choose it around pools.

Is travertine slippery around a pool?

Tumbled travertine has a naturally textured surface that stays grippy when wet. We recommend tumbled or honed finishes for pool decks, never polished.

Can you replace my existing concrete pool deck?

Yes. Most of our Orlando pool decks replace a cracked or faded concrete deck. We demolish, re-grade and rebuild in 4 to 7 working days.
