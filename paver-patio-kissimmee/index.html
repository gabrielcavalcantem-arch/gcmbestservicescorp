In Short

The backyard you actually use, twelve months a year.

Kissimmee homes come with generous lots and short winters, which makes the patio the most-used room of the house. GCM designs the layout first — seating, dining, fire feature, summer kitchen — then builds it in travertine, porcelain or Belgard pavers on an engineered base with proper drainage, so the surface stays level through Florida rain. We extend existing lanais, build freestanding terraces and tie the patio into the pool deck. Serving Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Celebration, Poinciana and Hunters Creek.

Frequently Asked

Questions worth asking.

How much does a paver patio cost in Kissimmee?

Paver patios in Kissimmee start around $10 per square foot installed. Travertine, porcelain, borders and fire features add to the total. Every project gets a fixed written quote after a site visit.

Can you extend my existing lanai or pool deck?

Yes. Extending a lanai or tying a new patio into the pool deck is one of the most common projects we build in Kissimmee and St. Cloud. We match or contrast the existing surface by design.

How long does a patio take?

A 300 to 600 sq ft patio is usually finished in 3 to 5 working days after materials arrive. Patios with kitchens, pergolas or fire features run 1 to 3 weeks.

Do you serve St. Cloud and Celebration?

We do — Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Celebration, Poinciana, Harmony and the rest of Osceola County, with HOA submittals handled for you.
