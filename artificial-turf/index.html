In Short

Green in August. Dry after the storm. Clean after the dog.

Florida kills natural grass twice a year: summer heat and the rainy season. Artificial turf solves both, but only when the base is built right. GCM removes the sod, installs a compacted aggregate base with proper slope and drainage, lays premium turf with a natural thatch layer, seams it invisibly and infills for the use you need — pet turf with antimicrobial infill, a putting green with sand infill, or a cool-touch lawn for a pool deck. We install artificial grass across Orlando, Dr. Phillips, Windermere, Lake Nona, Winter Park, Kissimmee and St. Cloud, and we handle HOA submittals.

Frequently Asked

Questions worth asking.

How much does artificial turf cost in Orlando?

Installed artificial turf in Orlando is priced by the square foot and depends on the turf grade, the base work and access to the yard. Pet systems and putting greens cost more than a standard lawn. We measure on site and give a fixed written quote — no estimate ranges that change later.

Is artificial turf good for dogs?

Yes. We install pet-specific turf with a fully permeable backing, a free-draining base and antimicrobial infill, so urine drains through and rinses away. It stays cleaner than natural grass and never turns to mud.

Does artificial grass get hot in the Florida sun?

Standard turf gets warm in direct afternoon sun. For pool decks and play areas we specify cool-touch yarns and lighter infill that run noticeably cooler, and we design shade and rinse points into the layout.

How long does artificial turf last in Central Florida?

Premium turf installed on a proper base lasts 15 to 20 years in Florida. Most failures you see around Orlando are base failures — wrinkles, low spots and standing water — not the turf itself. That is why we build the base like a paver driveway.

Can you install turf around a pool or over an old lawn?

Both. Pool surrounds are one of our most common turf projects; we tie the turf cleanly into travertine or paver coping. For existing lawns we remove the sod and roots completely before building the base.

Do you serve Kissimmee, St. Cloud and Lake Nona?

Yes. We install artificial grass across Orlando, Kissimmee, St. Cloud, Celebration, Lake Nona, Dr. Phillips, Windermere, Winter Park and Horizon West.
