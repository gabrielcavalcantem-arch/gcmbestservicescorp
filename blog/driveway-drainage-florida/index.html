Driveway Drainage Done Right in Florida

In Florida's heavy rains, drainage is what keeps a driveway performing for decades. Here is how it is done right.

Slope & grading

Correct slope directs water away from the home and prevents pooling on the surface.

Base & channel drains

An engineered base and, where needed, channel drains manage water so the driveway stays stable and dry.

Frequently Asked

Why does driveway drainage matter in Florida?

Heavy rain can pool and undermine a poorly drained driveway. Proper slope and base keep it stable for decades.
