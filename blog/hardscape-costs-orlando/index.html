What affects your price in Orlando

The biggest drivers are material grade (travertine, Belgard Luxury and marble cost more than standard options), total square footage, and base preparation. Custom patterns, borders, drainage, lighting and site access - common considerations in Orlando''s estate and gated communities - also influence the final figure.
